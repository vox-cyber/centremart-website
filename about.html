About Centre Mart

A shop the FJ family started in 2022, so nobody in Palakkal would have to drive to town for groceries.

Our doors opened on 28 November 2022. What began as a small supermarket inside Misha Shopping Complex has grown into the everyday grocery stop for hundreds of families across Palakkal, Chevoor, Snehapuram, and the wards around.

Our story

Rooted in Palakkal. Serving Thrissur.

Before 2022, most families in this ward drove to Thrissur city or Kodungallur for a proper weekly grocery run. That was a lot of petrol and a lot of time for something that should be simple.

So the FJ family — long-time residents of Palissery — put together a full-range supermarket right at the Palakkal Centre stop. Not a fancy hypermarket, not a corner kirana. Something in between: everything you need, at MRP or less, with a real person behind the counter who knows your name.

Three years in, that's still the whole plan. We don't want to be everywhere. We just want to be the shop Palakkal keeps coming back to.

A short timeline

Three years, one small mission

Nov 2022

The doors open

Grand opening on 28 November 2022 inside Misha Shopping Complex, right off the Kodungallur–Shornur Road.

2023

Home delivery, over WhatsApp

Started free delivery within the Palakkal ward — no app, no signup, just a message to the store number.

2024

The aisle count grows

Range expanded past 3,000 SKUs — baby care, personal care, imported oils, Kerala specialty foods.

2025

Contactless checkout everywhere

All counters now accept UPI, cards, and tap-to-pay. Cash still works, of course.

2026

Online, finally

This website — our first — so new neighbours can find us before they walk in.
